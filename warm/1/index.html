What this wedding is

Bear's side is a large Italian family and the reception turns into a proper Italian party — Tarantella and other Italian dances around 9:00. Brittney's own words for the look she wants are “Italy in Newport”: soft and timeless early, big and loud later.

Big wedding party — 15 groomsmen and 6 bridesmaids. Maple the dog is the flower girl. Live violinist at cocktail hour, In-N-Out truck at the end of the night.

The camcorder

The camcorder isn't just the 9:00 well-wishes — keep reaching for it through the whole day. It's a deliberate look, not a backup camera: grainy, soft, like an old home movie.

Especially anything with Jayden. That's the footage he'll be watching when he's grown, and the home-movie texture is exactly why it'll feel like a memory instead of a wedding video. Wide first so you can see where you are, then push in.
